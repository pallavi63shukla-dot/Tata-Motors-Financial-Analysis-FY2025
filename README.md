🔍 Key Findings

Revenue

Revenue increased by 1.31% in FY2025, indicating modest top-line growth.

Profitability

EBITDA margin declined from 14.10% to 13.11%, while PAT margin declined from 7.33% to 6.40%, indicating weaker operating and bottom-line profitability.

Working Capital

DSO improved from 13.75 to 12.53 days, while DIO increased from 59.24 to 63.83 days. DPO increased from 107.12 to 121.99 days, resulting in a more negative CCC of -45.62 days.

Capital Efficiency

ROCE declined from 18.70% to 17.60%, indicating a modest deterioration in capital efficiency.
