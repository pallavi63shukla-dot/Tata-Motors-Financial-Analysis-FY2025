# Tata Motors — Financial & Business Analysis FY2024–FY2025

## Project Overview

A financial and business analysis of Tata Motors based on FY2025
consolidated annual results.

The project evaluates financial performance, profitability,
working capital, business drivers, competitive positioning,
key risks and management priorities.

## Objectives

- Analyse FY2024 vs FY2025 financial performance
- Evaluate profitability and cash generation
- Analyse working capital efficiency
- Understand key business and revenue drivers
- Compare Tata Motors with selected peers
- Identify key risks and growth opportunities
- Develop an analyst perspective from financial data

## Key Metrics Analysed

- Revenue
- EBITDA
- EBITDA Margin
- PAT
- PAT Margin
- Auto Free Cash Flow
- DSO
- DIO
- DPO
- Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)

## Key Findings

- Revenue increased 1.31% in FY2025.
- EBITDA declined 5.79%.
- PAT declined 11.50%.
- Auto Free Cash Flow declined 17.00%.
- DSO improved by 1.21 days.
- DIO increased by 4.59 days.
- DPO increased by 14.87 days.
- CCC improved from -34.13 days to -45.62 days.
